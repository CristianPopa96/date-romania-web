# Date România: the site

Date România is a free public website that shows **where Romanian public money goes**.
It reads data that the state already publishes (public procurement, programme payments
and more), puts the pieces side by side, and shows every figure with the source it came
from. Where the published data stops, the site says so instead of guessing.

This wiki explains the site: what you can do with it, how to read what it shows, and
how to run it yourself. The data side (where the numbers come from, how they are
counted and linked, the API) has its own wiki in
[date-romania-api](https://github.com/CristianPopa96/date-romania-api/wiki).

## In one paragraph

A citizen types the name of their town hall and sees what it bought, from whom, and for
how much. A journalist opens the rankings to see the largest contracts and the companies
that win the most. A local councillor follows a road or water project paid by the
Anghel Saligny programme from the ministry down to the company that builds it. A
developer downloads the lists as CSV files. On every page, each number says which public
source it comes from and on which day it was read, and a gap in the public data is drawn
as a gap.

## Where things stand

As of 17.10.2026 the site runs locally and is not public yet (no domain). It is built
on a fixed set of data: **1 September to 10 October 2026**. Releases 1 and 2 of the
plan are built: search, institution and company pages, contract and direct purchase
pages, rankings with one pattern, the Anghel Saligny programme trace, and the sources
page with the error report form. See [Features and roadmap](Features-and-Roadmap).
