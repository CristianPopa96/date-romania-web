# Date România

**Date România is a free website that shows where Romanian public money goes.** It
gathers what the state already publishes (public purchases and contracts, town hall
budgets, payments from a national investment programme), puts the pieces side by side,
and shows each number together with the public source it comes from.

You don't need any technical knowledge to read this wiki. It explains what the platform
is for, what you can find on it, and how to read what it shows. Instructions for running
or changing the code are in each repository's README.

## In a few lines

- A **citizen** types the name of their commune and sees its town hall's budget, the
  contracts signed by the institutions there, and the companies that won them.
- A **journalist** opens the rankings to see the largest contracts, the companies that
  win the most, and patterns worth asking questions about.
- A **local councillor** follows a road or water project paid by the Anghel Saligny
  programme from the ministry down to the company that builds it.
- **Anyone** can download the lists, check where a number comes from, or report an
  error.

## Where things stand

As of 18.10.2026 the site runs on the maintainer's computer and is not public yet. It is
being built on a fixed slice of data: purchases and contracts from **1 September to 10
October 2026**, town hall budgets for **2024**, and the Anghel Saligny payment files
published up to **27 August 2026**. Three of the planned releases are built. See
[Features and roadmap](Features-and-Roadmap).

The data can be reused freely under the CC BY 4.0 licence, naming the source. The code is
open source.
